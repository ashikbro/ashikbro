## What I care about

I like building software that does real work without the usual AI nonsense:

- Tools that work from what you actually have (no invented experience, no hallucinated facts)
- Knowledge systems that stay on your machine and remain yours
- Interactive experiences and little technical toys that are fun to poke at
- Projects that are honest about where they are — clear status, real architecture notes, and privacy taken seriously

Most of what you’ll find here is still early, but the cores are working and the direction is deliberate.
